# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Poster browser: covers, quality / IMDb rank / series badges, year and A to Z tabs.
- Title pages with backdrop, Play button, player switch and favorites.
- Series: season tabs, episode list, "Continue S01 E03", the rest of the season queued as a playlist.
- Search across every library (about 36,000 titles), with Movies / Series and per-library filters.
- Search index bundled in the exe so search works on first launch; refreshed in the background when older than a day.
- Home page: continue watching, favorites, library tiles with online / offline state.
- App icon and version info.

## 0.1.0

- First version: folder browser for the DhakaFlix h5ai listings, play in VLC / PotPlayer / custom player.
